Jellycap

Plays a recap before episodes on Android TV. Add the recap folder below as a Jellyfin library (type: Movies or Home Videos, no metadata downloaders).

When the JellyXR TV app runs on the box, it draws the text cards over the player. Text recaps pause the episode for the cards (no video to build). Both recaps play the clips with the cards on top.

TV shows

Seasons / episodes: e.g. 1,3-5 or 2- (from 2 on). Empty = all. Season 0 = specials. Episodes are numbers within the allowed seasons, e.g. 2- skips the first episode of every season.
